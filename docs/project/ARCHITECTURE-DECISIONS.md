## Architecture decisions

The [Next Version decision record](../next/architecture/ADR/README.md) contains
future proposals and their status. A design or decision record is not evidence
that a component is implemented, supported, or released.

Dated release-time decisions and evidence remain in the
[historical release archive](../release/README.md). Current installation and
support boundaries are in [release status](RELEASE-STATUS.md) and
[implementation status](IMPLEMENTATION-STATUS.md).
